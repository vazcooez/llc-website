About Vazcooez

We build software
that earns the right
to be used.

Why we exist

Most working days are made up of small frictions: the inbox that no one owns, the workaround that became permanent, the tool that asks for clicks it shouldn’t need. Each one is small. Together, they take the day.

Vazcooez is a small software engineering company. We build calm tools that quietly remove those frictions — then get out of the way.

How we work

We use modern AI throughout our build process, including Claude. That is what lets us ship a product from idea to deployment in roughly two weeks, with the same craft a larger team would take a quarter to deliver.

We don’t ship for the sake of shipping. Every product is tested against a short list of words we don’t want anywhere near your day:

  • frustrate
  • distract
  • inconvenience
  • dislike
  • complex
  • felt the pain
  • friction
  • workarounds
  • difficult
  • block

What we’re building

Our first product, Xinbox, lets you describe each Slack channel in plain English and uses AI to route every incoming email to the right one — so teams stop triaging and start replying.

More is on the way. Get in touch if you want to hear about it first.
